# Mapnik - lockscreen map: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the Android app "Mapnik - lockscreen map" (the "app") uses, why it uses it, where it goes and how you can control it. It applies to the app as distributed on Google Play, including the Mapnik Pro subscription.

## In short

- The app has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
- Your Timeline (location history), GPX files and settings are kept on your phone. I never receive them.
- To show maps, find addresses and calculate routes, the app sends only what each request needs – for example the start and end of a route – to my own servers in the United Kingdom. The servers answer the request and do not store your IP address or a history of your requests.
- Mapnik Pro is paid for through Google Play. I never see your card or bank details.
- I do not sell or share your personal data.

## 2. Information that stays on your phone

The following information is created and used only on your device. It is kept in the app's private storage and is not sent to me or to anyone else, except as described in sections 3 and 8 or when you choose to share it (section 5).

- **Location:** your precise location is used to show where you are on the map, in the app and on the lock screen. When you ask for a route from where you are, your position is sent as the start point (see section 3).
- **Timeline (Mapnik Pro):** if you turn Timeline on, the app records your journeys in the background – location points with times, and the type of travel it detects (for example walking, driving or train). Turning Timeline off stops the recording.
- **Physical activity:** when you turn Timeline on, the app asks for the Activity recognition permission so it can tell walking from travelling in a vehicle. This is analysed on the phone only.
- **Your content:** routes, pins and waypoints you create, GPX files you import, and your settings. GPX files you export are saved where you choose.
- **Search cache:** recent address searches and their results (the questions sent to the search server and its answers) are cached for up to 90 days, so repeated searches are faster and fewer requests are sent.
- **Map data:** map tiles, offline map regions you download and public transport data are cached so the map loads faster and works offline.
- **Subscription status:** whether Mapnik Pro is active and when Google Play last confirmed it, so Pro keeps working for up to 7 days when Google Play cannot be reached.

## 3. Information sent to my servers

To show maps, search for addresses and calculate routes, the app connects to my servers at mapnik.duckdns.org. I run these servers myself, and they are located in the United Kingdom. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The requests do not contain any account, name, advertising ID or other identifier of you or your device.

- **Map data:** vector map tiles, shaded-relief tiles and public transport data. The app requests the map squares ("tiles") for the area you are viewing and for regions you choose to download. It may also fetch public transport data for the area around you and, when Timeline is on, for areas you have travelled through, so it can show nearby lines and recognise train and underground journeys. A tile request reveals only the general area (the map square) that is needed, not your exact position.
- **Routing:** when you ask for a route, the app sends the start point (which may be your current location), the destination, any waypoints and the travel mode.
- **Address search:** when you search for an address or place, or look up what is at a point on the map, the app sends the search text or the point's coordinates, together with a map position used to show nearby results first.
- **Technical data:** as with any internet connection, the servers receive your device's IP address and standard technical details of the request, such as the app and Android version.

How the servers use this information:

- Each request is used only to send the answer back to your phone and is then discarded. The servers do not keep access logs, do not store IP addresses and keep no history of the areas you view, your searches or your routes.
- I do not combine this information with other data, and I do not use it to identify, profile or track you.

## 4. Google Play and payments (Mapnik Pro)

- Mapnik Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. Google processes your payment and payment details under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). I never receive your card or bank details.
- The app checks whether your subscription is active directly with Google Play on your phone (Google Play Billing). No purchase information is sent to my servers.
- Google gives me limited information about each order in the Google Play Console – such as the order number, product, price, date and status, and, in sales reports, the country and approximate location (for example city or postcode area) used for tax. I use it only to manage orders and refunds, to answer your requests and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

## 5. Sharing – only when you choose

- When you export or share a GPX file, a route or your location, the app hands it to the app or person you select through Android's share function. From then on it is handled under that app's or person's own terms.
- I do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and I do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or analytics companies.
- I would disclose information only if the law required it. Because the servers keep no request logs, there is normally nothing to disclose.

## 6. The lock screen and the accessibility service

- The app shows a map on your lock screen. Anyone who can see your phone's screen, even while it is locked, may see the map, your current position, a route you are following or your Timeline track. You can stop the map appearing on the lock screen at any time by turning off the app's accessibility service (see below).
- To draw the map over the lock screen, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service API. It uses this service only to show and hide the lock-screen map. It does not read the content of your screen, what you type or information from other apps, and it does not collect or send any data through this service. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

## 7. Permissions

- **Location (precise):** to show your position and start routes from it – while the map is displayed in the app or on the lock screen, and in the background only when Timeline is on.
- **Physical activity (Activity recognition):** only for Timeline, to detect the type of travel.
- **Accessibility service:** only to show the map on the lock screen (see section 6).
- **Internet access:** to download maps and to send search and routing requests (see section 3).
- **Files:** GPX import and export use Android's file picker, so the app can open only the files you select.

You can withdraw any permission in Android Settings. Only the features that need it will stop working.

## 8. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data (for example settings and Timeline) in the backup stored in your Google Account. This backup is managed by Google, and I have no access to it. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.

## 9. Legal bases (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

- **Contract:** to answer the map, search and routing requests you make, and to provide Mapnik Pro.
- **Legitimate interests:** to keep the servers secure and working.
- **Legal obligation:** to keep order records for tax and accounting.

## 10. How long information is kept

- **On your phone:** until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Search results are cached for up to 90 days.
- **On my servers:** not kept. Each request is discarded once it has been answered.
- **Order information from Google Play:** as long as UK tax and accounting rules require.
- **Messages you send me:** as long as needed to answer you and deal with any follow-up.
